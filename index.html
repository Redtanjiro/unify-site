About

The art of bringing people together

Unify is an events and experiences house. We pair the warmth of ceremony with the calm of a team that has thought of everything.

Our name is our promise. The monogram joins U, n and Y beneath a single star: separate letters, one mark. That is what a celebration asks of its families, and what we ask of ourselves.

We work for couples and families planning from near and far, who want the day to feel rooted in tradition and still run like clockwork.
