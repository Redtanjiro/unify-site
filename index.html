Many planners sell beautiful weddings. Few sell certainty.

Couples abroad don’t lack inspiration. Their worry is coordination: a multi-day wedding across time zones, currencies, vendors, guests and families, with no one they can meet in person. Our answer is a single accountable team in India.

The meaning behind the moment

Four traditions, four ways to say I do

Hindu

Saptapadi

Seven steps taken together around the sacred fire, each one a vow.

Sikh

Laavan

Four rounds of the Guru Granth Sahib as the four hymns of the Laavan are sung.

Muslim

Nikah

The marriage contract, offered and accepted before witnesses.

Parsi

Ara Antar

A cloth held between the couple is lowered, and they see each other as husband and wife.

We work with priests and elders from each tradition, so every ceremony follows your family’s practice.

Behind every function.

Flowers checked against the design at dawn. Power tested before the first guest arrives. Sound checked with every band and officiant. The weather call made days ahead. Vendors tracked against the run-of-show, and every guest counted out and counted back.
